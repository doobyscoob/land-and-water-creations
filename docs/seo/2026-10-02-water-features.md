## Interview facts and editorial decisions
Materials and build time determine custom pond estimates; no starting price or general installation timeframe is promised.
The team draws top and side views, incorporates revisions, selects materials/plants and obtains final approval.
Living ponds need consistent care. Pondless waterfalls have substantially less upkeep but still need debris removal and algae management.
Orangedale Pond used roughly three truckloads of Tennessee boulders and took months. Monthly service is preferred for this particular pond, not prescribed for every installation.
Wading was an initial client goal. No swimming suitability or water safety claim is made.
Chemical products, dosage instructions, claims that UV stops bacterial growth, pollutant-free water, health benefits and guaranteed ecological outcomes were omitted. Technical equipment is described without unsupported performance claims.
